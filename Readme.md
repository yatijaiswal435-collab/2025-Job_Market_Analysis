# 📊 Job Market Analysis Dashboard — 2025

### Turning 2025 job-market data into insights for better career and hiring decisions

## 🔎 Project Overview

The **Job Market Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive data analytics project built in **Microsoft Excel** to analyze the **2025 job market**.

The project transforms job-listing data into meaningful insights about **job demand, salaries, locations, industries, work modes, experience levels, and employer-requested skills**.

The purpose of this analysis is to understand patterns in the **2025 employment market** and identify information that can support better career and hiring decisions.

## 🎯 Business Question

> **What can job-market data tell us about employment opportunities, salary patterns, locations, work modes, industries, and skills in 2025?**

The dashboard looks at the market from both a **job-seeker** and **business-analysis** perspective.

## 💡 What Does This Dashboard Analyze?

- 💼 **Job Demand:** Which roles appeared most frequently?
- 📍 **Job Locations:** Where were opportunities concentrated?
- 💰 **Salary Trends:** How did salary vary by experience?
- 🏢 **Industry Demand:** Which industries had more listings?
- 🌐 **Work Mode:** What was the Remote, Hybrid, and On-site distribution?
- 🧠 **In-Demand Skills:** Which skills were frequently requested?

## 📊 Dashboard Highlights

- **Total Jobs:** 420
- **Average Salary:** 7.74 LPA
- **Most In-Demand Job Role:** Sales Executive
- **Leading Job Location:** Bengaluru
- **Work Mode Analysis:** Remote vs Hybrid vs On-site

> **Note:** These figures represent the specific dataset analyzed in this project and are not a complete representation of the entire Indian job market in 2025.

## 📈 Key Areas of Analysis

### 2️⃣ Jobs by Location

### 3️⃣ Remote vs Hybrid vs On-site

### 4️⃣ Average Salary by Experience Level
Shows how average compensation changes across experience levels.

### 5️⃣ Jobs by Industry
Identifies industries contributing the largest number of analyzed job opportunities.

### 6️⃣ Most Requested Skills
Highlights skills frequently requested by employers in the analyzed listings.

## 🔍 Key Findings

- **Sales Executive** appears among the most frequently listed roles.
- **Bengaluru** is one of the leading locations in the analyzed data.
- Average salary generally increases across higher experience levels.
- Remote jobs represent a smaller share of the analyzed listings compared with other work arrangements.
- The skills analysis provides insight into capabilities frequently mentioned by employers.

> These findings are specific to the dataset used for this project and should not be generalized to every employer or job posting in India during 2025.
